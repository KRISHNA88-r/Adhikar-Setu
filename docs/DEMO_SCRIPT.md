# 3-minute demo flow

1. **Home** (10 s): "Anyone can speak a question in their language and learn what they are entitled to." Switch language to Telugu.
2. **Check my benefits** (80 s): consent -> answer by voice (age, woman, village, farmer, owns land, income) -> confirm "I heard..." -> results. Point at the reasons, an *Almost eligible* card ("bring an income certificate") and the yearly value.
3. **Scheme page** (30 s): tick documents, press *Print or save as PDF* for the application packet.
4. **Is this message real?** (30 s): paste: `PM Yojana approved! Pay registration fee Rs 500 within 24 hours: bit.ly/abc123` -> High risk with reasons. Then paste `https://pmkisan.gov.in` -> official.
5. **Understand a notice** (30 s): photo of a government letter -> summary, deadline, next action.
6. Close: "Rules decide, AI explains, every answer has a source, nothing personal is stored."
